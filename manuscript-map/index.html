How my papers build on one another. Each arrow points from a paper to a paper that builds on it, and each connection is broken down into the specific ways the two contribute to each other: results used or sharpened, methods reused, questions taken up, and more. Click a paper or an arrow to see the details, or filter the connections by type.

What the types mean

Arrows point from a paper to the paper that builds on it, so later work sits lower; a double-headed arrow is a two-way connection.

How this map was made

Each connection comes from a close reading of the papers themselves: where one cites another, reuses a lemma or construction, generalizes a setting, sharpens a bound, or takes up an open question. Theorem and lemma names under “Show details” refer to the papers; for manuscripts that are not yet published they are the labels in the current draft.

How this map was made

Each connection comes from a close reading of the papers themselves: where one cites another, reuses a lemma or construction, generalizes a setting, sharpens a bound, or takes up an open question. Theorem and lemma names refer to the papers; for manuscripts not yet published, they are the labels in the current draft.
